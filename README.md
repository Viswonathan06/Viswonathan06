## About

I'm a researcher and engineer working at the intersection of LLM alignment, AI fairness, and multi-agent systems. I'm currently a visiting researcher at UT Austin collaborating with [Prof. Desmond Ong](https://cascoglab.psy.utexas.edu/desmond/index.html) on sycophancy and empathy in LLMs, and previously a Research Assistant at the [Society-Centered AI Lab](https://neilgaikwad.github.io/) (UNC Chapel Hill) under Dr. Neil Gaikwad.
